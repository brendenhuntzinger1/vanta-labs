---

## The framing

Nothing here guarantees a sale. What a daily routine guarantees is **the input** that produces
sales: qualified strangers arriving at a store that can prove its standards.

**The target: 50–100 qualified visitors per day.** 8 orders from 364 lifetime visitors. A store
converting in the normal 1–2% range needs 50–100 visits/day to average one order/day. Measure days
in *visits created and proof published*, not in sales. Sales are the lagging indicator.

Total daily time: **~3h35m.**

---

## Block A — Build the proof (55 min, ends in 19 days)

The only finite block. In 19 days it is done forever.

1. **File two batch reports into the COA library** (30 min) — `coa_records` is empty; 1 of 38 live
   products has a report link. Two/day clears the catalogue in under three weeks. Until this exists,
   every "third-party tested" line on the site is unsubstantiated and the documentation creative
   archetype cannot be used.
2. **Complete two product pages, front to back** (25 min) — all 38 live products lack `seo_title`,
   `cas_number` and `peptide_sequence`; 4 have no image. Long descriptions are already written.

## Block B — Manufacture the traffic (70 min, indefinitely)

Two posts a day, not one. Finding a hook is a numbers game. Paid is closed until the category
ruling returns, so this is the entire top of funnel.

3. **Shoot two short-form videos** (45 min) — one always documentation archetype; the second rotates
   (cinematic macro, packing/cold chain, how an assay works, catalogue tour).
4. **Post both, everywhere connected** (15 min) — 9:16 and 4:5 from one shoot. Every link carries a
   UTM. 5,836 of 5,839 page views currently have no source.
5. **Write tomorrow's two hooks before closing the laptop** (10 min) — vary one axis at a time.

## Block C — Talk to actual humans (50 min)

At three customers, this is almost certainly where order nine comes from.

6. **Ten personalised approaches to bulk and lab buyers** (30 min) — `/wholesale` has 55 views from
   9 people with no follow-up path. Needs no ad platform's permission.
7. **Answer three real sourcing questions in public** (20 min) — with documents, never a pitch.

## Block D — Catch what you already have (30 min)

8. **Clear every inbound within the hour** (15 min).
9. **Email any new abandoned cart yourself** (10 min) — 9 carts all time, 29 automated emails, no
   recovery figure. At this scale, write it by hand and ask what stopped them.
10. **Add one subscriber** (5 min) — 5 subscribers is the entire owned audience. Offer the batch
    reports, not a discount.

## Block E — Record, then leave it alone (10 min)

Exists to stop you doing damage.

11. **Log five numbers** (5 min) — visitors, add-to-carts, orders, revenue, new subscribers. Filter
    admin traffic first; `/vault` and `/admin` are currently in the same analytics stream.
12. **Write one sentence on what you learned** (5 min) — in 60 days this is the most valuable
    marketing document you own, because it records what already failed.
13. **Change nothing today based on today** (0 min) — a hook needs ~2,000 impressions before a weak
    CTR means anything; conversion needs ~100 clicks. Decisions happen weekly.

## Weekly — Sunday, 90 minutes

- **Review** the week's seven rows together. Pick exactly one change.
- **Send** one email to the list. No promotion required (no coupon is live).
- **Publish** one research article, targeting questions asked in Block C.
- **Fix**: place one test order through a real ambassador link. 131 referral clicks have produced
  zero referral orders and zero commissions — genuine zero, or silent failure?
- **Ask**: talk to one past customer. Request a quotable review; ask what nearly stopped them.

---

## Guardrails — what "whatever it takes" must never mean

- **No outcome, transformation or before/after claims.** No dosing, no reconstitution, no "your
  results", no addressing the reader as the user.
- **No invented reviews, testimonials, ratings or COAs.** Three real customers exist — ask them.
- **No paid spend before the category ruling.** Four direct competitors checked; none running ads.
- **No discount invented to force a sale.** Margin funds the COA library, which is the differentiator.
- **No bought traffic, followers or engagement.** It corrupts the only signal the routine generates.
- **No working around a rejection.** Ineligible means a different concept, not a disguised one.

## What to expect

| Window | Reality |
|---|---|
| Days 1–14 | Probably no new orders. Normal, not failure. Judge on items ticked. |
| Days 15–30 | Catalogue finished and documented. First outperforming hook; first Block C inbound. |
| Days 31–60 | Search begins contributing. Repeat purchases show — 3 customers/8 orders is already the strongest signal in the data. |
| Real failure signal | Not a quiet week — 30 days of consistent execution with no movement in visitors. Then the hooks are wrong, and the log says which. |
