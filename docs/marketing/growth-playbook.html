The finding that reorders everything

Your coa_records table has zero rows. Of 38 live products — 35 of them in stock — exactly one carries a coa_url. Meanwhile your saved brand description — the one your ad tooling will draft from — leads with "third-party batch testing and transparent COA documentation."

That is the single strongest asset this brand has, it is the one angle in this category that survives ad review because it makes no health claim at all, and right now it is not substantiated. Until reports are filed, every "tested", "verified" or "transparent" line in your marketing is a claim without a source — which is both the compliance problem and the competitive one, because the whole differentiator is that a customer can go and check.

Second finding, from a live ad-library check: of four direct peptide sellers verified this morning — Precision Peptide Sciences, ProvenPeptides, Amino Asylum and Science.bio — none is currently running Meta ads. Only adjacent supplement brands (Seed, Thesis, Huel) are. Treat that as evidence about the category, not a gap in the market. It is why paid media sits at #47, not #1.

Execution

What to actually do first

Fifty tactics is a map, not a to-do list. This is the order that unblocks the most.

Week 1

#01–#05. Publish COAs for your top SKUs, resolve the coupon table, filter admin traffic out of analytics, tag outbound links, connect one social channel. Nothing here needs budget.

Week 1

#06 and #43. Submit for platform category review, and place one test order through an ambassador link to find out whether referral attribution is broken or genuinely at zero. Both are questions you cannot answer by reasoning.

Weeks 2–3

#07, #08, #24, #25. Wire the report library into every product page and fill the technical and SEO fields. This is the compounding work, and it is mostly data entry against a schema you already built.

Weeks 3–4

#13–#20. Shoot the first documentation and cinematic-product batch, post organically, test hooks one axis at a time. Cheap creative learning while the platform ruling is still outstanding.

Ongoing

#09, #30, #31. The batch-drop loop: every new batch produces a report, a post and an email. Operations generating marketing, so the content pipeline never depends on inventing topics.

Only after

#47–#50. Paid media, gated on the category ruling and on the COA library actually existing. Spending before either is settled buys data you cannot act on.
