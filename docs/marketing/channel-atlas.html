Every way there is to market this, and which ones are actually open to you

A complete catalogue — paid, organic, print, physical, partner, outreach, broadcast. The list itself is generic; the verdict beside each entry is not. Selling research-use-only materials closes doors that are wide open to an ordinary store, and the expensive mistake is discovering that after you have built the campaign. Filter by verdict to see what you can actually use.

Open

No category barrier. You are limited by effort and compliance of the message, not by anyone's permission.

Restricted

Usable, but with a real chance of rejection, account loss, or a partner who makes a claim you cannot make.

Unverified

Genuinely unknown for your category. Needs a written answer before you spend. The honest default here.

Closed

Policy almost certainly prohibits it. Attempting it repeatedly risks the account, not just the campaign.

One verdict changed while I was building this

I told you earlier to treat paid search as unknown. Live Google Keyword Planner data now says something more specific: buy peptides online carries High competition at $2.24–$9.16 top-of-page CPC, bpc 157 for sale High at $2.27–$6.34, and research peptides for sale returns real cost data too. Competition metrics reflect advertisers actually bidding.

Someone is buying these clicks. That is meaningfully better evidence than I had, and it is why Google Search sits at Unverified rather than Closed below. Two honest caveats: part of that competition is adjacent collagen-supplement advertisers, who sell a legally different product and are clearly present in the data; and advertisers appearing in an auction is not proof they are compliant or that they will still be there next quarter. It raises the question from "probably impossible" to "worth getting a written ruling on" — which is exactly what #06 in your playbook does.

Also worth knowing: research peptides draws 14,800 US searches a month at Low competition. That is an organic search opportunity sitting in plain sight, and it needs nobody's approval at all.
