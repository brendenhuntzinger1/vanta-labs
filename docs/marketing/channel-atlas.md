## Verdict key

| Verdict | Count | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| **Open** | 111 | No category barrier. Limited by effort and by the compliance of the message, not by permission. |
| **Restricted** | 39 | Usable, with real risk of rejection, account loss, or a partner making a claim you cannot make. |
| **Unverified** | 10 | Genuinely unknown for this category. Get a written answer before spending. |
| **Closed** | 15 | Policy almost certainly prohibits. Repeated attempts risk the account, not just the campaign. |

Verdicts are informed judgements, not legal or policy advice. Platform policies change and are
enforced inconsistently.

## The verdict that changed

The growth playbook told you to treat paid search as unknown. Live Google Keyword Planner data
(US, English) says something more specific:

| Keyword | Avg searches/mo | Competition | Top-of-page CPC |
|---|---:|---|---|
| research peptides | 14,800 | **Low** | $2.92–$11.33 |
| buy peptides online | 5,400 | High | $2.24–$9.16 |
| best place to buy peptides online | 4,400 | High | $1.96–$7.05 |
| bpc 157 for sale | 1,600 | High | $2.27–$6.34 |
| research peptides for sale | 1,300 | Low | $1.64–$5.96 |
| tirzepatide research peptide | 720 | High | $4.16–$10.90 |

Competition metrics reflect advertisers actually bidding, so **someone is buying these clicks**.
That moves Google Search from "probably closed" to **Unverified — worth a written ruling**.

Two caveats: part of that competition is adjacent collagen-supplement advertisers (clearly visible
in the data as "sports research collagen" etc.), who sell a legally different product; and
advertisers appearing in an auction is not proof they are compliant or durable.

Separately: **"research peptides" at 14,800/mo and Low competition** is an organic opportunity that
needs nobody's approval.

Max, Amazon, eBay, Etsy/Walmart, TikTok Shop, Instagram/Facebook Shopping, Pinterest and Snapchat
ads, streaming audio, national TV, push-notification networks.

### Notable openings people miss

business cards for trade shows and lab outreach, white-label supply, university and institutional
outreach, newsletter and podcast sponsorships bought direct from the host with no ad platform in
between, and the entire search and content stack.

## Constraint that travels

Products are laboratory research materials, research use only. No method above may imply human use,
dosing or reconstitution, or claim any medical, therapeutic, performance or body-composition
outcome — and that constraint travels to every affiliate, influencer and partner acting on your
behalf. A partner's outcome claim creates your exposure, not theirs.
